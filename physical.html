Physical exercise is a good way to calm down and reduce stress. This is because physical activity releases mood-enhancing chemicals, such as dopamine, serotonin, and endorphins. Here are a few exercises that will help you.

Go on a 20-minute walk

Walking reduces stress and lowers the risk of depression. It can also lead to improved cognitive function.

Practice yoga and/or tai chi

Mind-body practices lower cortisol, and can even strengthen areas of the brain that are responsible for emotional regulation and memory.

Go on a 10-minute run

Running helps with mood and stength, and can build neurological resilience by releasing Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor, which supports memory, focus, and the growth of new brain cells.

Try other activities that you may find fun, such as cycling or swimming
